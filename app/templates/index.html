Schedule recurring income & bills Jun 2026
  • New "Scheduled" page: Set up your paychecks and recurring bills once — pick the frequency (including semi-monthly with two pay days) and they post to your History automatically on each due date.
  • No more back-dated surprises: A schedule starts from the next upcoming date and never drops an entry on a day that already passed — so first-time setup stays clean.
  • Add transaction is just for one-offs now: The recurring options moved out, so logging a single purchase is simpler.
Add a transaction by just typing it Jun 2026
  • Plain-English quick add: Type something like "spent 42 on groceries at Safeway yesterday" in the box above and Budget Buddy fills in the transaction form for you — amount, category, account, and date.
  • You're always in control: It pre-fills the form for you to check and tweak — nothing is saved until you add it. An assist, not autopilot.
  • Right where you need it: The box is here on the home page, and again on the page where you add a transaction.
Edit anything in place — no page reloads Jun 2026
  • Inline edit & delete: Transactions, categories, accounts, budgets, and goals all edit right in the list — the row turns into a form, you save, and it swaps straight back. No more jumping to a separate page.
  • Quick confirmation: Every save or delete pops a small toast so you know it landed, and the page never fully reloads underneath you.

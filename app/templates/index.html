Account transfers & savings goals Jun 2026
  • Transfer money between accounts: record a move from one account to another in one step — both balances update, and the transfer stays out of your income and expense charts so it doesn't look like new spending or earnings.
  • Savings goals: set a target on an account and watch your progress fill in. Budget Buddy shows how much to set aside each month to hit your date, plus a projected finish based on your recent pace and an on-track / behind badge.
  • They work together: a transfer into a goal's account advances the goal automatically — no separate step.
More flexible recurring transactions & cleaner analytics Jun 2026
  • New recurring schedules: weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly (two pay days a month), monthly, quarterly, and annually — so things like bi-monthly paychecks fit properly.
  • "Exclude from analytics" toggle: mark opening balances or corrections so they still count toward your account balance but don't distort your spending and income charts.
  • Fix: recurring transactions no longer duplicate over time.

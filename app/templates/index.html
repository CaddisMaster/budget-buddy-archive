A more honest Safe to spend
  • Keep savings out of it: Every account now has a "Count in Safe to spend" toggle — untick your savings account and that money stops looking spendable. Scheduled transfers into it count as money leaving your pool, too.
  • Card bills count now: Bills that charge to a credit card used to reserve nothing. Now they're counted like any other upcoming bill — unless you have a recurring payment transfer already covering that card.
  • Same promise, better math: The number still means "cash you can spend before your next paycheck" — it's just no longer flattered by savings balances and invisible card charges.

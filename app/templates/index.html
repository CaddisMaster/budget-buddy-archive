Goals can now pay off debt
  • Pay a balance down to $0: A goal can now target clearing a debt — pick "Pay off a balance", choose the account (say, a credit card), and Budget Buddy tracks your progress from what you owed down to zero.
  • Know your monthly number: Give the goal a date and it tells you exactly how much to set aside each month to be debt-free on time — and, from your recent payments, when you're actually on pace to get there.
  • Honest progress: "Still owed" always reflects the account's real balance — if new charges land, the goal adjusts instead of pretending it didn't see them.
Balance check-in
  • Match the bank in one click: On the Accounts page, hit "Check in", type what the bank actually says, and Budget Buddy posts the one adjustment that brings its balance in line with reality.
  • Charts stay honest: The adjustment fixes the balance without polluting your spending analytics — it's marked as a correction, not a purchase.
  • See what's gone stale: Every account now shows when it was last checked in, so you know which balances you can trust at a glance.
Safe to spend
  • One honest number: The dashboard now answers "can I afford this right now?" — your cash minus the bills, transfers, and budget room already spoken for between now and your next paycheck.
  • See the math: Expand the breakdown to see exactly what's coming out and when — every upcoming bill and transfer, each with its date.
  • Red means stop: If what's committed outruns your cash, the number goes negative and shows it — no wishful rounding.
Budget report
  • Budgets get memory: The Budgets page now shows your last six complete months per category — green where you stayed under budget, red where you went over.
  • Streaks and trends: See how many months in a row you've stayed under (or over), and whether each category's spending is rising, easing, or steady.
  • Honest grading: Past months are judged against each category's current budget, and only budgets you've actually set get a verdict.

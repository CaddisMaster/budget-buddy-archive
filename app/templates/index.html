Let AI right-size your budgets Jul 2026
  • One review pass: On the Budgets page, hit "Review" and Budget Buddy looks at your last six months of spending — where you consistently run over, where a budget sits well above real life — and proposes a right-sized monthly amount for each category, with a plain-English reason.
  • You stay in control: Proposals come back as a checklist — edit any amount, uncheck anything you don't want, then confirm. Nothing is ever applied automatically.
  • Grounded in your numbers: Every figure starts from your own history, and each proposal is kept within a sensible range of what you actually spend.

Clean up uncategorized transactions Jun 2026
  • Suggested categories, in one pass: When transactions land without a category, your History page shows a "Review" button — Budget Buddy reads them and proposes the best-fitting category for each, from your own category list.
  • You stay in control: Suggestions come back as a checklist with a confidence note — change any category, uncheck anything you don't want, then confirm. Nothing is ever applied automatically.
  • Catches the odd ones too: It also flags the occasional already-categorized transaction that looks mis-filed, so your spending breakdowns stay accurate.

A weekly recap in your inbox
  • Budget Buddy comes to you: Turn on the weekly email digest and get a Sunday-evening recap — how your spending compares to budget, where your net position stands this month, and what's scheduled to come in or go out over the next 7 days.
  • You choose to receive it: It's off until you opt in on your Profile page with your email address — set it once and the digest arrives every week.
  • Plain-English, grounded in your numbers: Every figure is your own; Budget Buddy just writes it up in a friendly recap with a tip or two.
Savings goals, refreshed — with an AI coach
  • A cleaner look: Your goals — on the Goals page and the dashboard — got a fresh coat of paint that matches the rest of the app, plus a new "overall progress" bar showing how much you've saved toward everything at once.
  • Meet your Goal Coach: One button reads how each goal is tracking — what's on pace, what's fallen behind, and roughly when you'll get there — and writes it up in plain English with a nudge or two to close the gap.
  • Your numbers, your call: Every figure is computed from your own accounts; the coach only narrates it, and it stays hidden until you have a goal in progress.

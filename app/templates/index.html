v10.10.0 In progress
{# ── One block per feature in this release. Add / remove blocks to match. ── #}
A more honest Safe to spend
  • Keep savings out of it: Every account now has a "Count in Safe to spend" toggle — untick your savings account and that money stops looking spendable. Scheduled transfers into it count as money leaving your pool, too.
  • Card bills count now: Bills that charge to a credit card used to reserve nothing. Now they're counted like any other upcoming bill — unless you have a recurring payment transfer already covering that card.
  • Same promise, better math: The number still means "cash you can spend before your next paycheck" — it's just no longer flattered by savings balances and invisible card charges.
Credit limits on your cards
  • Set a limit, see your headroom: Give any credit card its limit on the Accounts page and the card shows a utilization bar — amber past 30%, red past 80% — plus exactly how much credit is left.
  • One total across cards: A summary line adds up the available credit on every card with a limit set.
  • Your AI knows it too: Ask "how much room is left on my card?", and the monthly Insight and weekly digest can flag a card creeping toward its limit.
The Money agent — a weekly check on your money
  • An AI investigator on your side: Once a week it combs your recent activity for things worth a second look — duplicate charges, a bill whose amount changed, a card creeping up on its limit, a budget on pace to blow.
  • Evidence or it doesn't count: It reports at most three findings, each backed by the actual dates and amounts from your own data — and it only reads, never changes anything. Most weeks the answer is simply "nothing notable."
  • On the dashboard and in your inbox: A new dashboard card shows the latest check (run it yourself anytime), and the weekly email digest includes the same findings.

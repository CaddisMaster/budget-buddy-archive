v10.12.0 2026-07-12
{# ── One block per feature in this release. Add / remove blocks to match. ── #}
Safe to spend retired
  • Simpler dashboard: The "Safe to spend" band and its breakdown are gone — the number wasn't earning its place, so the hero now leads with income, expenses, and net.
  • Simpler accounts: The "Count in Safe to spend" checkbox is gone from the account forms too — one less thing to think about when adding an account.
Income categories
  • Categories have a kind now: Each category is an expense or an income category (set it when you create or edit one) — so labeling your salary, freelance, or interest income finally does something.
  • Income breakdown on the dashboard: The "Spending by category" chart has an Expenses / Income toggle showing where your money comes from.
  • Cleaner budgets: The budget page only lists expense categories — no more dead "Income $0" row — and category dropdowns group the two kinds.
  • Smarter Ask: Asking "how much did I make from X" now sums what came in for an income category instead of answering $0.
History balance fixed
  • The running balance connects across pages now: It used to restart from zero on every History page; each row now shows the true running total of everything older, and filtered views carry their own running net page to page.

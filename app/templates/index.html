A fresh new look Jun 2026
  • A redesigned dashboard: The Dashboard now opens with an at-a-glance summary of where you stand — your net position for the month, with income and expenses right beside it — above cleaner, easier-to-read charts.
  • Clearer navigation: The sidebar has icons and a tidier layout, so finding your way around is quicker, and everything shares a more polished, consistent style throughout.
  • Same app, more refined: Nothing moved or changed how it works — it just looks and feels sharper, in both light and dark mode.
Clean up uncategorized transactions Jun 2026
  • Suggested categories, in one pass: When transactions land without a category, your History page shows a "Review" button — Budget Buddy reads them and proposes the best-fitting category for each, from your own category list.
  • You stay in control: Suggestions come back as a checklist with a confidence note — change any category, uncheck anything you don't want, then confirm. Nothing is ever applied automatically.
  • Catches the odd ones too: It also flags the occasional already-categorized transaction that looks mis-filed, so your spending breakdowns stay accurate.
Set up automatic transfers Jun 2026
  • Move money on a schedule: On the Transfer money page, set up a recurring transfer once — say $200 from checking to savings on the 1st of every month — and Budget Buddy posts it for you automatically each time it's due.
  • Weekly to yearly: Pick any rhythm — weekly, bi-weekly, twice a month, monthly, quarterly, or annually — and edit or remove it any time.
  • Going forward, never back-dated: It only ever posts from the next due date on, and each one records the same paired transfer you'd enter by hand, so both balances move and it stays out of your spending analytics.
